Talking metronome demo v13 (recorded counts)

A prototype that plays recorded spoken counts on the audio clock, the same clock as the clicks, so every word lands where it belongs. Not part of Melodies by Ear yet.

Experimental. Each word is a short recording. When words are closer together than the recording is long, "Squeeze words to fit" plays a quicker version of the word (same pitch); anything still too long fades out as the next count starts.

Default is 67% (triplet swing).

Default is on time. Each word is already lined up so its vowel lands on the count; use this only if, on your device, the words all feel a little ahead of or behind the clicks.
